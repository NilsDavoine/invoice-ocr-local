# Results

_Generated by `python -m invoice_ocr report` from `results/*.json`. Do not edit by hand._

Two sets of fictitious invoices, FR / EN / DE, about half degraded as scans (rotation up to 2°, blur, noise, JPEG), 10 % deliberately inconsistent (they must come out as `TO_VERIFY`):

- **Tuning set** (`python -m invoice_ocr generate`, seed 2026, 40 invoices `INV-xx`, layouts A/B/C): the extraction rules were written and adjusted on it. Anomalies: wrong total, 20 % rate, broken VAT number checksum, due date before issue date.
- **Held-out test set** (`python -m invoice_ocr generate --holdout`, seed 7351, 30 invoices `TST-xx`): generated after the rules were frozen and never used to change them. Six new suppliers, new invoice number formats, and a layout D the rules never saw (metadata as a grid with labels above values, supplier VAT number only in the footer, VAT summary beside the totals). Anomalies: wrong total, broken VAT number checksum, due date before issue date.

## Simulated misreads (how much the checks catch)
